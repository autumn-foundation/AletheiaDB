**Single-Pass Collection & Vector Alignment**
**Learning:** When transitioning from a two-pass collection strategy to a single-pass loop with lazy columnar allocation, it is crucial to understand the cardinality of each property. Node-specific vectors (e.g., `nodes`, `properties`) align with `node_count`, while generic row attributes (e.g., `scores`, `paths`, `versions`) align with the total `row_count`. Early termination (e.g., `continue`) based on a specific entity type will drop data for other fields.
**Action:** Always introduce a dedicated `row_idx` variable to track the total number of iterations and use it to correctly pad non-entity-specific vectors, ensuring `_ => {}` (fallthrough) is preserved instead of `continue`.
