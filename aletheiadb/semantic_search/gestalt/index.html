Gestalt: Semantic Subgraph Matching Engine.

“The whole is greater than the sum of its parts.”

Gestalt finds occurrences of a specific graph pattern where nodes are matched not just by label, but by semantic similarity to a concept vector.

This enables queries like: “Find a [Person ~ ‘Engineer’] connected to a [Company ~ ‘Startup’] via [WORKS_FOR].”

§Concepts

  • Pattern: A template subgraph with constraints.
  • Anchor: A node in the pattern with a vector constraint, used to seed the search.
  • Match: A concrete subgraph in the database that satisfies the pattern.
