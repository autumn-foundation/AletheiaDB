Archetype: Semantic concept extraction and purity scoring.

§The “Nova” Philosophy 🌟

Archetype answers the question: “What is the Platonic ideal of these nodes, and how well does each node embody it?” By calculating the centroid of a set of vectors (the Archetype), we can score the semantic “purity” of individual members, finding outliers or core representatives.

§Use Cases

  • Concept Extraction: Identifying the central theme of a group of articles.
  • Anomaly Detection: Finding nodes that claim to be part of a group but lack semantic similarity.
  • Representative Sampling: Finding the most “pure” examples of a cluster.
